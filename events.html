SCOPE BEFORE COMMITMENT

Prepare the partnership
Define the delivery

Does this include on-site event production?

On-site production, venue operations, staffing and safety remain with the event organiser and appointed local providers unless separately agreed and resourced. The initial discussion focuses on research, planning and partnership communication.

Can you guarantee attendance or a sponsor?

No. Invitations, expressions of interest, confirmed attendance, paid sponsorship and actual delivery are separate outcomes. None is guaranteed by a research task.

What should an event brief include?

The event format, date, location, audience, available budget, responsible organiser and support needed. For sponsorship, include the rights you are authorised to offer and any category exclusions.

How is remuneration agreed?

YourBloom’s service fee or any commission is agreed separately in writing. Any sponsorship fee or commission is agreed in writing before work begins.
