Enquiries and individual agreements

This website introduces the areas in which YourBloom can discuss project support. Browsing the site, emailing an enquiry or using the demonstration form does not create a purchase, booking, representation agreement or obligation to pay. Services require a separate written agreement.

Scope and remuneration

Before work begins, the parties must agree the scope, deliverables, responsibilities, fees or commission, payment terms, content rights and cancellation arrangements. No standard commission or fixed agency tariff is established on this website. Creator fees and other campaign costs require their own explicit agreement.

Creator and partner relationships

Introducing a profile does not guarantee paid work or establish exclusive representation. Campaign reach, engagement and sales are not guaranteed. Event and sponsorship support is scoped to the work expressly agreed.

Illustrative materials

Editorial portraits, planning examples and sample briefs are illustrative. They are not presented as client campaigns, an exclusive creator roster, testimonials or achieved results.

Existing studio services

The design catalogue and course pages remain available separately. Ask for current availability and written project terms before relying on older service descriptions or prices. No payment is collected by the agency pages.

Contact

Questions about a potential project can be sent to hello@yourbloom.site.
