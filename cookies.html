Local website assets and clear information about browser storage.

Agency pages

The agency frontend does not set cookies or save enquiry entries in local storage, session storage or a browser database. Fonts and images are loaded from this website.

Hosting and protection

Cloudflare hosts and protects the website. The enquiry service uses technical request information to limit frequent submissions. Your browser may independently retain page state or offer autofill; you can control these features in its settings.

External services

Form submissions are delivered to YourBloom through Telegram. Email and social links open separate services with their own policies. Read the privacy notice for information about enquiry handling.

Existing studio pages

Older catalogue and course pages retain their existing scripts and settings and may use browser storage or analytics separately from the agency pages. You can inspect and clear site data through your browser settings.
