PRIVACY NOTICE

Who to contact

YourBloom is the founder-led studio presented by Maria on this website. For questions about information you share, email hello@yourbloom.site.

Information in an enquiry

The form asks for your name, email, target market and project brief. Depending on your enquiry, you may also provide public profile links, company details, timing, budget context or collaboration preferences. Optional fields can be left empty. Do not send passwords, banking details or identity documents.

Where your enquiry goes

When you submit the form, your enquiry is sent to Formspree, a third-party form processing service, for delivery to YourBloom’s email inbox so Maria can review and respond. Formspree processes this information under its own privacy practices. Cloudflare hosts the website and processes technical request information.

Use and retention

YourBloom uses the information to respond, assess project fit and discuss possible work. Any preference for future collaboration opportunities is optional and separate from the enquiry. Messages are kept in the working inbox while needed for these conversations and any resulting project. Contact YourBloom with a request to correct or remove information you have shared.

Website protection

The form checks required fields in your browser, while Formspree provides its own submission handling and spam protections. The website does not intentionally save your form entries in browser storage; your browser may independently offer autofill or retain page state.

Email and existing studio pages

Email links open your own email application. You decide whether to send a message. Older course pages retain their existing services and settings, including separate progress features. Contact YourBloom if you need clarification before using them.
