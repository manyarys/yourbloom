How YourBloom handles website enquiries and written communication.

PRIVACY NOTICE

Who to contact

YourBloom is the founder-led studio presented by Maria on this website. For questions about information you share, email hello@yourbloom.site.

Information in an enquiry

The form asks for your name, email, target market and project brief. Depending on your enquiry, you may also provide public profile links, company details, timing, budget context or collaboration preferences. Optional fields can be left empty. Do not send passwords, banking details or identity documents.

Where your enquiry goes

When you submit the form, the website sends your enquiry through a server-side Telegram bot to YourBloom’s configured inbox so Maria can review and respond. Telegram processes the message under its own privacy practices. Cloudflare hosts the website and processes technical request information to deliver and protect this service. The enquiry handler does not write submissions to the existing course database or Google Sheets.

Use and retention

YourBloom uses the information to respond, assess project fit and discuss possible work. Any preference for future collaboration opportunities is optional and separate from the enquiry. Messages are kept in the working inbox while needed for these conversations and any resulting project. Contact YourBloom with a request to correct or remove information you have shared.

Website protection

The enquiry handler checks the information provided and limits frequent submissions. Technical request information is used to help prevent abuse. The form does not save your entries in browser storage; your browser may independently offer autofill or retain page state.

Email and existing studio pages

Email links open your own email application. You decide whether to send a message. Older design catalogue and course pages retain their existing services and settings, including separate enquiry or progress features. Contact YourBloom if you need clarification before using them.
