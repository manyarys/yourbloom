PRIVACY NOTICE

Who to contact

YourBloom is the founder-led studio presented by Maria on this website. For privacy questions about these agency pages, contact hello@yourbloom.site.

The demonstration brief form

The form prepares a readable brief on the current page. It does not send your entries to an inbox, spreadsheet, CRM or other server, and it does not save them in cookies, local storage or a database. Use sample details when exploring the demonstration. Clear the preview or close the page to remove the displayed brief. Your browser may independently retain page state or offer autofill.

Email and external services

Email links open your own email application. You decide whether to send a message. Email and social platforms process communications under their own policies. Keep an initial enquiry to the information needed to discuss the project; do not send passwords, banking details or identity documents.

Hosting and local assets

The site is hosted through Cloudflare. Delivering and protecting a website involves technical connection information such as an IP address and request details. The agency frontend loads its fonts and images locally and does not activate analytics, advertising pixels, payment services or the existing studio enquiry handler.

Existing studio pages

The existing design catalogue and course pages remain available. They use their existing scripts and services; the local-only description of the agency demonstration form does not describe those separate flows. Contact YourBloom before providing information through an older page if you need clarification about its operation.

Questions and future changes

Contact YourBloom with privacy questions or requests about information you have shared directly. Any future live form or additional service must be described here before it is enabled.
