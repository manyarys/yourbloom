What if I only need one part?

Start with a defined task: a profile review, a content plan, a media-kit draft or support for a specific enquiry.

Can we work entirely online?

Yes. Briefs, feedback and approvals can be handled in writing. We agree how confidential materials are shared before starting.

Does working together guarantee followers or brand orders?

No. The work has defined deliverables and a review process. Audience growth and commercial results depend on fit, execution and the market.

What does the service cost?

The fee or commission depends on the agreed work. Send your situation and support needs so I can suggest a useful scope.

Should I take the course first?

The course is an independent learning option. Individual support can be discussed separately; purchasing the course is not a condition for making an enquiry.
