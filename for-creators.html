A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Know where
you stand.

Does an introduction guarantee a brand deal?

No. A profile is considered in the context of a specific brief, budget and brand fit. An introduction does not guarantee an opportunity or a paid contract.

Will YourBloom become my exclusive manager?

No exclusive management relationship is created by sharing a profile. Any representation or separate service would need its own written agreement.

Do I need to publish after being invited to an event?

An invitation alone is not a content agreement. Any required post, attendance commitment, fee or usage right should be agreed separately in writing.

Who can introduce a profile?

This initial creator enquiry is intended for adult creators. Please use your own public profile and contact details, or explain your authority to act for the creator.
