What goes into
your shortlist

For each candidate, I show a relevant piece of work, explain why the audience and creative approach fit your brief, and flag missing evidence. A creator’s location alone tells us little about where their viewers live.

We agree the research scope, fee and timing before I start. If you want help with outreach or coordination, we define who contacts whom and who approves the terms. Availability, rates and usage rights need confirmation from each creator.
