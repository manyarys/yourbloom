BEFORE WE BEGIN

Clear scope.
Clear expectations.

How is pricing agreed?

Each proposal sets out the deliverables, responsibilities and YourBloom fee or commission in writing. Pricing is tailored to the scope; no standard agency percentage is published.

How are creator budgets handled?

The intended project structure is for the brand to agree and pay creator fees directly. YourBloom’s own service fee or commission is agreed separately. The proposal makes those responsibilities explicit.

Can you guarantee reach or sales?

No. Creator suitability, content quality and a clear brief support better decisions, but reach, engagement and sales depend on factors that cannot be guaranteed.

What information do you need first?

Your product or service, target market, campaign objective, preferred formats, timing and any creator budget already set. Share only information you are authorised to disclose.
