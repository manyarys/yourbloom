WORKING TOGETHER

A small scope
A proper process

Define the task

Share the brief, profile links, preferred format and deadline. Confirm that any client information can be shared for the agreed purpose.

Agree the boundaries

Set the deliverables, price, revisions, confidentiality and permitted use of the work. White-label arrangements are discussed explicitly.

Review the handoff

Receive the agreed findings with sources and limitations. Any outreach or creator booking remains a separate, approved step.

Do you contact creators as part of a shortlist review?

No. A review is a research deliverable. Sending messages, negotiating or booking creators requires a separate scope and approval.

Can the work be delivered under our agency brand?

A white-label arrangement can be discussed where your client agreement allows it. Confidentiality, attribution and permitted subcontracting need to be confirmed first.

What is the price of the review?

The price is confirmed in a written proposal for the agreed brief. Fees and any commission are agreed in an individual proposal before work begins.
